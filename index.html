What I do

Evidence generation from real-world data, from the first feasibility question to publication.

RWE study design & analysis

Retrospective studies with Kaplan-Meier and Cox models, interrupted time series, regression and concordance analysis.

Treatment patterns & persistence

Switching, persistence and discontinuation, healthcare resource use and real-world endpoints such as relapse and infections.

AI-enabled chart abstraction

LLM pipelines that turn clinical notes and lab reports into analysis-ready variables, validated for PPV, sensitivity and F1.

RWD for trial feasibility

Turning eligibility criteria into cohort definitions and attrition funnels to find and match patients for studies.

Selected work

ALZHEIMER'S · BIOMARKERS · CTAD

Diagnostic pathways to anti-amyloid therapy

Compared blood-based biomarkers, PET and CSF in routine care, found care gaps and the fastest route to anti-amyloid therapy, and mapped the patient journey from cognitive assessment to biomarker results.

ALZHEIMER'S · PERSISTENCE

Anti-amyloid therapy persistence

Kaplan-Meier analysis of real-world time to discontinuation, stratified by biomarker status, to find what drives patients to stop treatment.

MULTIPLE SCLEROSIS · TREATMENT PATTERNS

Disease-modifying therapy in real-world MS care

Led a retrospective study comparing DMT classes on switching, healthcare resource use and relapse, with real-world endpoints extracted from clinical notes by a validated LLM pipeline.

RESPIRATORY · ISPOR EUROPE 2023

COVID-19's impact on RSV and influenza

Interrupted time series models quantifying how RSV and influenza incidence changed before and after COVID-19 in the US and EU-5.

PYTHON · SYNTHETIC EHR · PERSONAL PROJECT

What real-world data can and can't tell you about trial eligibility

5,000 synthetic patients vs. a Phase 3 Alzheimer's protocol: 14.6% pass the checks an EHR can make; 5.5% are fully eligible.

COMMERCIAL ANALYTICS · CLAIMS

Launch planning & prescriber targeting

Treatment-pattern dashboards for an oncology portfolio, forecast inputs from claims, and HCP segmentation from prescription and distribution data.
